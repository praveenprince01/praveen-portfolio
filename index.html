About
A founder-led design practice focused on building useful digital experiences.

I currently run PPA Design Studio as a UI/UX Designer, Trainer, and Mentor — designing digital products for clients while helping students and working professionals build practical UI/UX skills, portfolios, and career-ready work. Day to day that means user research, wireframing, prototyping, design systems, and responsive web design, handed off cleanly for development — plus 1:1 training and mentorship built around real projects rather than theory. I also lean on AI-assisted workflows to move faster through ideation, documentation, and research synthesis.
